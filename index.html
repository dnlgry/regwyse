Advice for those who make regulation — and those who work within it.

RegWyse is an independent regulatory advisory firm working across the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.

We help governments and regulators design, reform and implement regulatory systems, and we help businesses understand, respond to and operate within them.

Our work sits at the point where policy, regulation and implementation meet. That can mean shaping a new regulatory framework, reviewing whether an existing regime is working, helping an organisation enter a new market, or translating regulatory change into practical governance, policy and operational requirements.

Governments & regulators

We support public bodies with regulatory strategy, policy development, reform, review and implementation.

Our work can include options analysis, regulatory design, consultation, governance, intergovernmental coordination, implementation planning and evaluation.

We focus not only on what a regulatory system is intended to achieve, but on how it will operate in practice: who will administer it, how regulated organisations will respond, where unnecessary burden may arise, and how outcomes can be measured.

Businesses & regulated organisations

We help organisations understand what regulation means for them in practical terms.

That includes market-entry advice, regulatory mapping, licensing and registration requirements, governance and compliance design, regulatory change, readiness assessments and engagement with regulators.

Our role is to establish what applies, what is material, what needs to change and how regulatory requirements should be translated into workable business arrangements.

Cross-border work

For organisations operating across multiple jurisdictions, we identify what is already covered by existing arrangements, what is materially different and what genuinely needs to change.

We compare regulatory regimes across the UK, Australia and New Zealand, identifying equivalence, gaps, duplication and jurisdiction-specific requirements.

The result is a clearer route into a new market without redesigning governance and compliance arrangements unnecessarily.

That means translating legislation, policy and regulatory expectations into clear decisions, governance arrangements, implementation plans and operational actions.

Cross-border regulation without starting again.

Organisations entering a new jurisdiction often already have substantial governance and compliance arrangements in place. Our role is to determine what can be retained, what needs to be adapted and what is genuinely new.

Market entry

We identify the regulatory regimes relevant to the proposed activity and translate them into a practical entry plan.

This may include regulators, licences, registrations, governance requirements, conduct obligations, reporting requirements, industry standards and sector-specific obligations.

Regulatory mapping

We compare requirements across jurisdictions to identify equivalent obligations, material differences, duplication and requirements unique to a particular market.

The result is a structured view of what the organisation already has and what it needs to change.

Implementation

We convert the regulatory analysis into a prioritised programme of governance, policy, control and operational change.

Where specialist legal, tax or technical advice is required, we work alongside appropriately qualified advisers.
