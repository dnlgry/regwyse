RegWyse is an independent advisory practice working with governments and organisations involved in the development, reform and implementation of public policy and regulation.

We support local, state, territory and Commonwealth governments in Australia, and local and central government in New Zealand. We also work with consulting firms, industry and business associations, think tanks, policy institutes and advocacy organisations.

Our work combines public policy expertise, regulatory knowledge and practical implementation experience. We help clients understand complex issues, assess options, engage stakeholders and develop solutions that are proportionate, evidence-informed and workable in practice.

Governments & regulators

We advise public-sector organisations across the policy and regulatory lifecycle, from identifying problems and developing options through to implementation and evaluation.

Our work includes regulatory design and reform, policy review, governance, consultation, institutional arrangements and intergovernmental coordination.

We bring an understanding of how government operates and the practical constraints involved in delivering effective policy.

Consultancies & research organisations

We provide specialist regulatory and public policy expertise to consulting firms, research organisations and policy institutes.

This may involve contributing to larger projects, leading specialist workstreams, conducting research or providing independent analysis and advice.

We offer flexible senior-level capability to complement existing project teams, particularly where regulatory knowledge, public-sector experience or policy judgement is required.

Industry, associations & advocates

We support organisations that represent industries, businesses, communities and other interests in the policy process.

Our work includes policy research, regulatory analysis, consultation submissions, advocacy strategy, stakeholder engagement and evidence-based policy proposals.

We help clients understand policy developments, articulate their positions and contribute constructively to regulatory decision-making.

RegWyse brings these perspectives together to support sound decisions and effective implementation.

Navigating complex policy and regulatory environments.

Public policy problems rarely fit neatly within one institution, sector or level of government. They often involve overlapping responsibilities, competing interests, regulatory constraints and difficult implementation choices.

RegWyse helps clients understand these complexities, identify practical options and develop informed, credible approaches to policy and regulatory change.

Understand the problem

We examine the policy context, available evidence, institutional arrangements and regulatory environment.

Our analysis helps establish the nature of the problem, its underlying causes, the interests involved and the opportunities for intervention.

Develop the options

We develop and assess policy and regulatory options, considering effectiveness, proportionality, feasibility and likely impacts.

We work with clients to identify appropriate approaches, articulate the evidence and build a sound basis for decision-making.

Support implementation

We translate preferred options into practical recommendations and plans for delivery.

This may include governance arrangements, institutional responsibilities, stakeholder engagement, programme design and implementation planning.

Australia and New Zealand have closely connected economies, institutions and policy traditions, but distinct systems of government, regulatory frameworks and public-sector arrangements.

RegWyse focuses on the policy and regulatory environments of both countries, bringing comparative insight, local knowledge and an understanding of how different levels of government approach common challenges.
